**Theme:** "The time is always right to do right. We are all tied together in a single Garment of Destiny caught in an inescapable network of Mutuality."

**Keynote:** Southern Oregon University President Rick Bailey

**Performers:** Students from SOU, Ashland High School, Ashland Middle School, Helman and Walker elementary schools, Nina Davis, Rogue Valley Peace Choir

After the celebration, march to downtown Plaza for the playing of Dr. King's "I Have a Dream" speech.

**When:** Noon–1:30pm, doors open 11:30am

**Where:** Historic Ashland Armory, 208 Oak St., Ashland

**Overflow:** OSF's Carpenter Hall (live stream)

**Admission:** FREE — non-perishable food donations encouraged for SOU Food Pantry
